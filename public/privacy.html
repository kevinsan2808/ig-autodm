Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

AutoDM is a private tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account it is connected to, to reply to comments and direct messages on that account.

What data we process

When someone comments on or sends a message to the connected Instagram account, we receive the comment or message text, the sender's Instagram-scoped ID and username, and the related post ID through the official Instagram API.

How we use it

This data is used only to generate and send a reply to that comment or message. We do not sell, share, or use it for advertising.

Storage

We keep a short activity log (recent events only) so the account owner can see what the bot did. Logs are not shared with anyone and are periodically cleared.

Third parties

Message text may be sent to an AI provider solely to draft a reply. No other third parties receive your data.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, send a direct message to the connected Instagram account with the word "DELETE". We will remove all related records within 7 days.
